# BrandKraf Guest-Post & PR Target List

Vetted, **legitimate editorial** outlets — not paid guest-post farms (those get you penalised; avoid anything
that says "$30 dofollow link" on Fiverr/PeoplePerHour/Outreach Dudes etc.). These are real Malaysian/SEA
publications where a genuinely useful article or expert quote earns a real, penalty-free backlink.

> Reality check: editorial placements are merit-based, not guaranteed. But one link from Vulcan Post or
> Marketing-Interactive is worth more than 100 farm links — and it sends real referral traffic and leads.

---

## TIER A — Lowest effort, fastest links (do these first)

You answer a journalist's question in a few sentences; if used, you get quoted with a link. No full article.
~15 minutes each, no pitching. **Best fit for a busy founder.**

| Platform | URL | How it works |
|---|---|---|
| Featured.com | https://featured.com | Free. Answer expert questions; get featured on high-DA sites. Start here. |
| Help a B2B Writer | https://helpab2bwriter.com | Free. B2B writers request quotes; reply to relevant ones. |
| Qwoted | https://www.qwoted.com | Freemium. Journalists from Forbes/Inc./TechCrunch etc. post requests. |

**Routine:** sign up for all three, set your topics to *digital marketing, social media, TikTok, SME marketing,
Malaysia*, and spend 15 min twice a week answering 1–2 relevant queries. Link to whichever guide fits the
question (usually [/guides/digital-marketing-basics](https://www.brandkraf.com/guides/digital-marketing-basics) or a specific tool).

## TIER B — Best-fit Malaysian editorial (highest relevance & authority)

These reach *your* audience — marketers, SMEs, business owners. Worth a tailored pitch.

### 1. Marketing-Interactive (A+M Malaysia) — ⭐ top pick
- **URL / section:** https://www.marketing-interactive.com/opinions
- **Audience:** Marketing & advertising professionals across Malaysia/SEA. The industry publication.
- **Angle:** A sharp industry opinion. e.g. *"Why UGC is quietly outperforming polished ads for Malaysian brands"* or *"TikTok Shop has changed the rules for Malaysian retail — here's what brands keep getting wrong."*
- **Link to:** [/guides/ugc-influencer-marketing](https://www.brandkraf.com/guides/ugc-influencer-marketing) or [/guides/tiktok-marketing](https://www.brandkraf.com/guides/tiktok-marketing)
- **How:** Submit via their Opinions/editorial contact. Write as the founder, expert POV, no hard selling.

### 2. BusinessToday Malaysia
- **URL / section:** https://www.businesstoday.com.my/category/opinion/
- **Audience:** Corporate Malaysia, business owners, decision-makers.
- **Angle:** Newsy & data-led. e.g. *"The SME Digitalisation Grant: are Malaysian businesses leaving RM5,000 on the table?"*
- **Link to:** [/blog/sme-digitalisation-grant-2026](https://www.brandkraf.com/blog/sme-digitalisation-grant-2026) (and the [cost calculator](https://www.brandkraf.com/marketing-cost-calculator))
- **How:** Pitch the opinion editor; lead with the timely grant hook.

### 3. Vulcan Post — ⭐ top pick
- **URL:** https://vulcanpost.com (guest author page: https://vulcanpost.com/author/guest-post/)
- **Audience:** Entrepreneurs, SMEs, consumers across Malaysia/SEA. High authority + real traffic.
- **Angle:** Founder-voice, story or data. e.g. *"What digital marketing actually costs a Malaysian SME in 2026 (with real numbers)"* or *"I run a marketing agency — here are the 5 mistakes I see Malaysian SMEs make."*
- **Link to:** [/blog/digital-marketing-cost-malaysia](https://www.brandkraf.com/blog/digital-marketing-cost-malaysia) or [/guides/digital-marketing-basics](https://www.brandkraf.com/guides/digital-marketing-basics)
- **How:** Email **team [at] vulcanpost [dot] com** with your pitch/angle.

### 4. SAYS (IMHO) — easiest entry, big reach
- **URL:** https://says.com/my/contributor
- **Audience:** Mass Malaysian social-media audience (huge, younger).
- **Angle:** Relatable / listicle. e.g. *"5 marketing mistakes every Malaysian small business makes"* or a festive piece.
- **Link to:** [/guides/social-media-marketing](https://www.brandkraf.com/guides/social-media-marketing) or [/blog/hari-raya-marketing-playbook](https://www.brandkraf.com/blog/hari-raya-marketing-playbook)
- **How:** Min ~300 words, submit via the Contributor page (IMHO). Free; they allow backlinks. **Good first win to build confidence.**

### 5. Smart Investor Malaysia
- **URL:** https://www.smartinvestor.com.my
- **Audience:** Business owners & finance-minded readers.
- **Angle:** Budget/ROI framing. e.g. *"How much should a Malaysian business spend on marketing to actually grow?"*
- **Link to:** [/blog/digital-marketing-cost-malaysia](https://www.brandkraf.com/blog/digital-marketing-cost-malaysia) + [/tools](https://www.brandkraf.com/tools)
- **How:** Pitch via their contribute/contact page (they openly invite guest writers on business/finance topics).

## Your reusable pitch (copy & adapt)

> **Subject:** Guest article idea: {specific title}
>
> Hi {name}, I'm {Founder name}, founder of BrandKraf, a KL-based digital marketing agency. I loved your recent
> piece on {topic}. I'd like to contribute a free, original article your readers would find genuinely useful —
> "{specific title idea}". It's practical and Malaysia-specific, no fluff or AI spam, with one relevant link to a
> free resource on our site. Happy to send a full outline first. Would that be a fit?

**Rules that get you published:** lead with value not promotion, make it Malaysia-specific, reference local
context/data, one natural link (not five), and follow each outlet's guidelines exactly.
