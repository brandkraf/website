# BrandKraf Backlink Playbook

A practical, no-cost-first plan to build authority links to **https://www.brandkraf.com**.
Work top-to-bottom — the early tiers are the easiest wins and also strengthen local SEO.

> **Golden rule:** Relevance + authority beats volume. 10 links from real, relevant sites
> outrank 1,000 spammy ones. **Never buy PBN / Fiverr "link blast" packages** — they get you
> penalised. Every link below is earned or self-submitted on a legitimate platform.

---

## Tier 3 — Earned links from your content (the compounding engine)

You now have **30 blog articles**. Some are natural "linkable assets" — data/guide pages other
people cite. Promote these specifically:

- **Digital Marketing Cost in Malaysia (2026)** — pricing/data pages earn links; people cite numbers.
- **SME Digitalisation Grant 2026** — local, high-intent; relevant for business blogs & forums.
- **UGC Creator Rates in Malaysia** — niche data other marketers reference.

**How to earn links to them:**
1. **Guest posting** — write for Malaysian marketing/business blogs; link back to a relevant guide.
2. **Digital PR / expert quotes** — respond to journalist requests (e.g. Featured.com, Help A B2B Writer,
   Qwoted). A quote in an article = a high-authority link.
3. **Community answers** — genuinely answer questions on Reddit (r/malaysia, r/MalaysianPF), Lowyat
   forums, Quora, and FB groups; link your guide only where it truly helps (no spam).
4. **Statistics/round-ups** — pitch your data points to "best of" and stats round-up posts.

## Tier 4 — Relationship & partnership links (underrated, high trust)

- **Clients** — add a tasteful "Marketing by BrandKraf" link in the footer of sites you manage.
- **Partners / suppliers / tools you use** — many vendors have a "customers" or "case study" page;
  offer a testimonial in exchange for a link.
- **Local associations & chambers** — SME Association of Malaysia, local business chambers, MDEC
  programmes, co-working spaces — member directories often link out.
- **Sponsorships / events** — sponsoring a local meetup or webinar usually earns a link.
- **Existing relationships** — vendors, your accountant, your web host's partner page, etc.

## ❌ What to avoid (these hurt you)

- Buying links, PBNs, or "1,000 backlinks for RM50" gigs.
- Mass blog-comment / forum-signature spam.
- Identical over-optimised anchor text everywhere (e.g. always "digital marketing agency Malaysia").
  Keep anchors natural and varied: brand name, URL, and descriptive phrases.
- Irrelevant directories (random global link farms with no editorial standard).
